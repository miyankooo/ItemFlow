# ItemFlow

## Unreleased

- Renamed from Fast Loot Sell Repair. Settings start fresh under the new name.
- Fast Loot now loots the way SpeedyAutoLoot does: the loot window stays hidden unless something needs you, so a spammed interact never opens an empty window.
- Buy Max Stack: right-clicking an item at a vendor buys a full stack. A checkbox in the vendor window turns it on.
- Mark Items to Drop: with loot left behind for lack of room, your cheapest stacks worth less than that loot glow, one per loot item, as LootTriage pairs them. With 2 or fewer free slots, the cheapest stack glows. Gray and white stacks get a red cross that deletes them.
- Sell Junk uses Blizzard's Sell All Junk and honours bags marked to ignore it.
- Opening the settings in combat opens them once combat ends.
